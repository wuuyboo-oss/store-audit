What the 12 checks cover

  1. llms.txt present — the file AI assistants read first to learn what you sell.
  2. robots.txt allows AI shopping bots — GPTBot and OAI-SearchBot must be allowed to crawl you.
  3. Product structured data (JSON-LD) — machine-readable product info on every product page.
  4. Price + availability in structured data — without these, an assistant cannot recommend or sell the product.
  5. Product feed exists — most ChatGPT Shopping picks now come from feeds, not crawled pages.
  6. Sitemap lists products — the fallback discovery path when no feed exists.
  7. Products render without JavaScript — crawlers that do not run JS see an empty page otherwise.
  8. Shipping + returns pages exist — assistants will not recommend a store whose terms they cannot read.
  9. Reviews / ratings markup — AggregateRating is a ranking signal.
  10. Image alt text — how an assistant "sees" your product images.
  11. Canonical URLs — stops duplicate product URLs splitting your visibility.
  12. Agent-completable checkout — express wallets and agent-checkout support decide whether the sale can finish.

Honest limits

  • The demo store scores 56/100 because it is checked with its homepage, a product page, robots.txt and sitemap together. Paste mode only sees the homepage, so pasting the demo's homepage alone scores lower — by design, not by error.
  • This is a lite checker. It cannot fully verify agent checkout (UCP/ACP) from a static page, and it cannot fetch stores that block cross-site requests — use the Paste HTML tab in that case.
  • A good score does not guarantee ChatGPT will recommend you; a bad score means it very likely cannot find you at all.
  • Traffic figures on this page are from Adobe Analytics (via Verlua, Aug 2026) and Profound (Sep 2026, vendor-run prompts, not real shopper sessions).
