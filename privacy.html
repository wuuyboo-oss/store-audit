Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026. Short version: the checker runs in your browser and we do not collect your store data.

The free checker runs entirely in your browser. 4. Contact

For any personal data held by our payment processor, please contact Dodo Payments directly. If you have emailed us and want your message deleted, write to the address above and we will delete it.
